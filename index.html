How we source
  1. Primary sources first: peer-reviewed papers, archives, official records.
  2. Where experts disagree, we say so in the video and list both sides here.
  3. WHAT IF episodes are thought experiments; sources cover the real facts they start from.
  4. Visuals are illustrations, never photographic evidence.
