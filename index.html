Short videos, long receipts.

Each episode on OGS Mystery Lab runs under three minutes, which leaves no room for footnotes. This page is the footnotes. Find your episode below, and you get the studies, archives, books and articles the script was built from — so you can check us, go deeper, or argue with us in the comments.

How we source

  1. Primary sources first: peer-reviewed papers, archives, official records.
  2. Where experts disagree, we say so in the video and list both sides here.
  3. WHAT IF episodes are thought experiments; sources cover the real facts they start from.
  4. Visuals are illustrations, never photographic evidence.
